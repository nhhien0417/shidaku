Privacy

Shidaku prototype. Last updated: 2026-09-09.

This is a playable prototype used to find out whether a game idea is worth building. It is not a released product and it has no user accounts.

What is collected

Anonymous gameplay events only: that a page was opened, that the game loaded and drew, that a rectangle was drawn correctly or not, which section was reached, whether a hint was used, how long a session lasted, and whether the "Get the full game" button was shown or tapped. Alongside those we record technical context - a random session identifier generated in your browser, approximate device class, connection type, screen size, and the campaign parameters in the link you arrived from.

We do not ask for or store your name, your account, your location, your contacts, or any advertising identifier. The random session identifier is not linked to you and is not kept after the browser session ends.

Session recordings

Some sessions are recorded as anonymised screen interactions (taps and movement on the game screen) so we can see where the game confuses people. Recordings contain no typed text and no personal data.

If you give us an email address

Only if you type it into the form on the "in development" page. It is used for exactly one thing: telling you once when Shidaku is available. It is not used for any other mail, and it is not sold or shared. Ask us to delete it at any time and we will.

Who processes this data

  • PostHog - gameplay analytics.
  • Microsoft Clarity - anonymised session recordings and heatmaps.
  • Meta Pixel - tells the advertising campaign that a visitor loaded the game, made a first move, and finished the first and third sections. No personal data is sent.

Storage on your device

Two values, both optional: your sound on/off preference (localStorage) and the random session identifier (sessionStorage, discarded when you close the tab). No game progress is saved - reloading starts a fresh session.
